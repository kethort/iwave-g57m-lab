This site is an independent bench notebook for practical Versal board bring-up. The emphasis is on observable behavior: exact tool versions, generated artifacts, serial output, failure boundaries, and recovery paths.

It is **not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by iWave Systems, AMD, or Xilinx**. Product names and trademarks are used only to identify the hardware and software being tested, and belong to their respective owners.

The notes do not redistribute vendor BSP archives, licensed toolchains, proprietary schematics, private support material, or unreleased documentation. When a workflow depends on vendor material, obtain that material directly from the vendor under the applicable license or support agreement.

Examples are provided for educational use. Review vendor documentation, your hardware design, and your license obligations before programming a device or publishing derived work.
